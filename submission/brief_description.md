# KisanSetu AI (किसान सेतु) — Brief Description

**KisanSetu AI** is a national interoperable digital agriculture network and open Digital Public Good (DPG) that delivers real-time, localised agro-advisories to Indian smallholder farmers using Google Gemini AI, satellite telemetry (ISRO Bhuvan/Sentinel), IMD weather forecasting, and Soil Health Card data — featuring regenerative crop recommendations, a multimodal crop disease diagnostic tool, multilingual voice support in 8 Indian languages, and an inter-state cooperative knowledge mesh enabling cross-border agricultural model sharing, early-warning federation, and climate-resilient farming aligned with India's AgriStack and Beckn open protocols.

---

## Three-Line Summary

KisanSetu AI is an AI-powered, interoperable digital agriculture platform for Indian smallholder farmers that provides hyper-localised regenerative crop advisories using satellite data, soil health cards, and IMD weather feeds, powered by Google Gemini. It includes a multimodal crop disease diagnostic tool (Crop Doctor Vision) that analyses farm images to identify diseases and prescribe organic/integrated remedies. Built as a scalable Digital Public Good with 8-language voice support and an inter-state cooperative knowledge mesh, it enables sustainable, climate-resilient food production across India.
