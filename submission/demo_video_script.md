## SCENE 2 — FEATURE 1: REGENERATIVE ADVISORY (0:30 – 1:30)

> "The banner instantly shows Punjab's agro-telemetry: current NDVI vegetation index, soil moisture, seasonal rainfall, and the primary agricultural challenge — water table depletion."

**[Fill the Soil Health Card form]**
> "Now I'll enter a typical Punjab Soil Health Card: Nitrogen 210 kg/ha — low, Phosphorus 18 kg/ha — medium, Potassium 185 kg/ha — optimal. pH 7.2, Organic Carbon 0.52%."

**[Click Generate Advisory]**
> "Clicking 'Get AI Advisory' — this calls Google Gemini with the full agro-climatic context. Watch the response..."

**[Show results: crop cards with scores]**
> "Gemini recommends: Basmati Rice as primary, with companion marigold for pest suppression. Sustainability score: 78/100. Water savings: 35,000 litres per hectare versus conventional paddy. And a 12-week calendar with weekly tasks."

**[Click SMS Export]**
> "Farmers without smartphones? Click 'SMS Export' — the full advisory compresses to 3 SMS messages in Punjabi or Hindi."

## RECORDING TIPS
- Use OBS Studio or Loom for screen recording
- Keep cursor movements smooth and deliberate
- Pause 1 second after each click for UI to respond
- Use the Quick Tour bar to navigate between features quickly
- For demo without Gemini API key: click "Demo Mode" in the GeminiConfigModal
- Recommended: Record 4.5 minutes and trim to 4 minutes in editing
